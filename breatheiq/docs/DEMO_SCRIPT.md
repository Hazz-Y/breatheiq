# BreatheIQ — 90-second demo script

Before you start: `npm run build && npm run preview`, open the Live page, press `P` and **Reset**, set speed **×2**, close the panel (`Esc`).

| Time | Do this | Say this |
|------|---------|----------|
| 0:00 | Live page, top of hero | "Train cabins are cramped, packed and badly ventilated. Air goes bad between stations, and nobody on board knows." |
| 0:10 | Scroll to the gauge + LCD | "This is our prototype in coach B2: temperature, two gas sensors and a laser dust counter on an Arduino Mega. The LCD and this console are exactly what the hardware prints." |
| 0:22 | Point at the four heads | "A Random Forest on the PC answers every frame. Four heads: temperature runs the exhaust fan; gas, CO₂ and dust ask for the ozone purifiers." |
| 0:32 | Press `2` | "Platform rush — doors open, dust and diesel come in." *(CO₂ head flips, fan comes on)* |
| 0:40 | Point at the yellow BLOCKED box | "The model wants ozone. But ozone is harmful to people, so our interlock refuses while the cabin is occupied, and falls back to fan and filtration." |
| 0:48 | Press `7` | "Now the worst day: heat wave, stubble smoke, a packed coach." |
| 0:56 | Wait for the red banner and hatched edge (about 15 s at ×2 — keep talking about the heads lighting up) | "AQI goes past 500. We don't clamp it, because this is the day that matters. Bad Day Mode arms itself." |
| 1:02 | Click **Open playbook** | "In seconds: ventilation boost, ozone checked by the interlock, an SMS to passengers who flagged asthma or pregnancy, a draft platform announcement, and a suggestion to move them to the cleanest coach." |
| 1:14 | Click **Exposure** in the top bar | "Same air, different risk. Children and asthma patients get their own dose, time to the WHO limit, and plain advice." |
| 1:24 | Point at the header chip | "SENSOR means real hardware today, SIM means what we build next. Plug in the Arduino on the System page and this goes live." |
| 1:30 | Press `1` | "BreatheIQ. Breathe easier between stations." |

Backup: if something stalls, `P` → **Reset** → `7` and narrate from 0:48.
